Google and GitHub sign-in now use PKCE (S256), a single-use `state` bound to the browser, and, for Google, full ID-token validation (RS256 signature via JWKS, `iss`, `aud`, `exp`, `iat`, `nonce`). Provider emails must be verified before they are used, and an OAuth identity is matched by provider and subject, never by email: it is linked to an existing local account only when that account's email is verified too (otherwise sign-in ends at `/login?error=oauth_link_refused`). The post-login `next` path must be a same-origin relative path, and production refuses non-https `GOOGLE_REDIRECT_URI` / `GITHUB_REDIRECT_URI`. Behaviour change: users who relied on unverified-email linking must verify their email or sign in with their password. See `docs/security/SSO_DESIGN.md`.
