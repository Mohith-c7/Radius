Worker jobs now keep a renewed lease with an owner: a long job is no longer re-claimed by another replica, and a worker that lost its lease cannot complete, fail or publish. Failed attempts retry with exponential backoff and the poller retries a failed head a bounded number of times. New settings: `WORKER_HEARTBEAT_INTERVAL_SECONDS`, `WORKER_JOB_RETRY_BASE_SECONDS`, `WORKER_JOB_RETRY_MAX_SECONDS`, `RADIUS_GITHUB_POLL_FAILED_RETRIES`, `RADIUS_GITHUB_POLL_FAILED_RETRY_DELAY_SECONDS`. Migration `20261103_0017` adds two nullable columns to `analysis_jobs`.
