Opt-in `RADIUS_INTENT_EXTRACTION_MODE=llm` proposes criteria from the PR title and body with a guarded model (proposed only, the verdict stays blocked until a human confirms) and opt-in `RADIUS_FEATURE_SUGGEST_TESTS` shows gated pytest suggestions in a separate "not run, not evidence" block. Both send PR text to a third-party provider and are off by default; the verdict is never computed by a model.
