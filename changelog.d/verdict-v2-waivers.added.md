Verdict v2 waivers (VD-05): `.radius/waivers.yml` (read from the base branch) accepts a tamper finding, a tamper rule within a path scope, or a criterion, with a required reason, owner and an expiry at most 90 days after creation. Expired, invalid and duplicate waivers are ignored and reported; waived findings are shown as WAIVED with reason and owner in the PR comment and the API; each verdict stores an audit record of the waivers applied; a contradicted criterion and a failed CI case can never be waived. Behind `RADIUS_FEATURE_VERDICT_V2`. See `docs/policy.md` and `.radius/waivers.example.yml`.
