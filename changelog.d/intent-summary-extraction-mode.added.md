Added the opt-in `RADIUS_INTENT_EXTRACTION_MODE=summary` (default `sections`): a deterministic mode that proposes low-weight, never-confirmed criteria from a conventional-commit PR title and behavioural bullets under Summary/Changes/What/Why/Description headings. While such a criterion is unconfirmed the Verdict v2 verdict cannot be `verified` (blocking degraded reason `unconfirmed_inferred_intent`). `scripts/verdict_v2_replay.py` gains `--intent-mode summary`.
